As global alignments continue to shift toward a multipolar architecture, middle powers are increasingly adopting flexible diplomatic strategies to safeguard their strategic autonomy. 

Navigating complex trade corridors, maritime security protocols, and rapid technological integration requires proactive policy frameworks. Rather than relying on traditional bloc alignments, modern statecraft demands dynamic multilateral partnerships across economic and defense sectors to preserve national stability and regional security equilibrium in an evolving geopolitical landscape.
